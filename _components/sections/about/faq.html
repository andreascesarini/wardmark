Frequently asked questions

How do you handle confidentiality?

I share a client’s name only with their explicit permission. When I talk about work from earlier roles, I describe it as professional experience.

Can you advise us before we choose a security product?

Yes. I work independently of vendors. I start with what your organisation needs to control, how your current systems are used and what your team can maintain. That gives you a basis for deciding whether to change a process, adjust an existing control or bring in a tool.

Can Wardmark help with one part of an existing IAM programme?

Yes. I can work on a defined area, such as reviewing administrator access or planning the identity work for a migration. We agree on how it fits with the work your team already has underway.

Can we work with you in English or Swedish?

Yes. I can work with your team in either language.
