What I learned before I went into IT

I spent six years in construction. I learned to pay attention to the foundations and to talk to the people whose work depended on mine. On a building site, getting the sequence right matters as much as getting your own part right.

As a group leader in Sweden’s 1st Amphibious Regiment, I learned to be clear about the task, who was responsible for each part of it and what needed to happen when plans changed. Those habits carry over into identity work. Before changing who can access a system, I want to understand what relies on that access and hear from the people who run it.
