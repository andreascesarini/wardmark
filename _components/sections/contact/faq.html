Frequently asked questions

What should I include in my message?

Tell us what you’d like to discuss and a little about your organisation. A few sentences are enough to start the conversation.

Can consultancy brokers get in touch?

Yes. Tell us a little about your consultancy and the kind of organisation you’re supporting.

Who will I hear from?

You’ll hear from Andreas Eriksson, Wardmark’s founder and the architect who leads and carries out the work.

Can I fill in the form in Swedish?

Yes. You can write in English or Swedish.
