Frequently asked questions

Can we start with a focused review?

Yes. A review can cover one area, such as administrator access in Active Directory or how a group of critical applications authenticates with Microsoft Entra ID.

Can Andreas work alongside our internal IAM team?

Yes. Andreas can work with your engineers on design, implementation and handover. Your team brings its knowledge of the environment and stays involved in the decisions it will own afterwards.

What do we get at handover?

For a review, you get a prioritised action list with the reasons behind each recommendation. When the engagement includes implementation, you also get a record of what changed and what your team needs to monitor or maintain.

Can we prepare for AI agents before choosing a platform?

Yes. You can decide who should own agent identities, who can approve their permissions and how their access will be reviewed before selecting a platform.

Can consultancy brokers join the waiting list?

Yes. Tell us a little about your consultancy and the kind of identity expertise you’re looking for.
