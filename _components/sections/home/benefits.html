How an engagement works

  1. Agree the scope.

    Decide which systems and access decisions need attention. Agree on what Andreas will review or implement and what your team will receive.

  2. Plan the work with your team.

    Review findings with the people who know the environment. When implementation is in scope, sequence changes around the applications and services that need to stay available.

  3. Hand over the decisions.

    Walk through the work with the people who’ll own it. They get the documentation they need to maintain access controls and make the next change.
