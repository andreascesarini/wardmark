Meet Andreas Eriksson

Before founding Wardmark in 2026, Andreas worked in identity and infrastructure across Sweden, Australia and Switzerland. His experience includes financial infrastructure, private banking, pharmaceuticals, and the defence and aerospace sector.

Six years in construction taught him to pay attention to the foundations before building on them. Serving as a group leader in Sweden’s 1st Amphibious Regiment taught him the value of a clear plan that people can carry out together. Both lessons shape how Andreas works with clients today.
