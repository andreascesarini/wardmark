Keep critical work running through times of change

A service account set up years ago may still run a critical application. Wardmark helps your team see dependencies like that before a migration, a change to someone’s permissions or the introduction of a new system.

  • Reduce unnecessary access.

    Find forgotten administrator accounts and remove permissions that have outlived their purpose.

  • Give people and systems the access they need.

    Define which permissions each role, service or agent requires and who is responsible for reviewing them.

  • Stand behind your access decisions.

    Give leadership and auditors a clear record of who and what has access, why it’s needed and who approved it.
