Where Wardmark can help

Active Directory & Entra ID Security

Review and harden your Microsoft identity environment. Identify which accounts hold privileged access, clean up unnecessary permissions and use relevant CIS Benchmarks to guide configuration changes.

Identity Architecture & Migration

Design and implement identity architecture using Zero Trust principles. Plan directory consolidations and migrations around the applications and teams that rely on the current environment.

Access Governance

Define roles, assign ownership and make access reviews easier to run. Keep a record of access decisions that your team can use for internal reviews and audit work tied to requirements such as ISO 27001, NIS2 and DORA.

AI Agent Identity Readiness

Decide what an AI agent should be allowed to do before it connects to business systems. Define the identity it runs under, what it can access and how your team will review its activity.
