Frequently asked questions

Can we start with one part of the organisation?

Yes. The work can focus on a defined area, such as contractor access, administrator roles or a group of business-critical applications.

Where do we start if administrator access is unclear?

An Active Directory and Entra ID security review can show which accounts and groups hold privileged access today. Your team can then agree which permissions are needed and who should be responsible for them.

Can the work support an audit?

Yes. Wardmark can help document what access was granted, who approved it and when it was last reviewed. Those records can support work tied to ISO 27001, NIS2 or DORA, depending on what your team needs to show.

Does access governance apply to service accounts and AI agents?

Yes. Service accounts and agents need an owner who can explain what they’re allowed to access and why. AI agent identity readiness helps your team set permissions, ownership and review expectations before an agent connects to business systems.
