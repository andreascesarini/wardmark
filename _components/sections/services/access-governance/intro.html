Make access decisions clear

When a manager moves from Finance to Operations, they need access for the new role. Your team also needs to know which permissions to remove and who can approve temporary access during the handover. Clear rules make those decisions easier to handle consistently.

  • Roles and permissions. Define which permissions go with each role and which need separate approval.
  • Ownership and approvals. Assign responsibility for approving access, reviewing it and handling exceptions.
  • Changes and reviews. Set out how permissions are updated when people join, change roles or leave, and when existing access should be reviewed.
  • Records. Document what was approved, who approved it and what changed after a review.
