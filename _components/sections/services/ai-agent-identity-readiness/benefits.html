Give your team a basis for approving the rollout

Andreas Eriksson works with the people responsible for the workflow and the teams that run the systems it will touch. You get a record of the proposed permissions and owners, along with a prioritised list of decisions and changes to make before rollout.

When implementation is in scope, Andreas puts the agreed access controls in place with your identity team. The handover gives them a record they can use when the agent’s job changes or its permissions come up for review.
