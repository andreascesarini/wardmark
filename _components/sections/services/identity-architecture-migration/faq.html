Frequently asked questions

Can we migrate in stages across on-premises and cloud environments?

Yes. The plan can sequence the move by application, directory or group of users. Each stage accounts for dependencies between Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID so your team knows what to check before moving on.

Can Wardmark design the architecture while our team handles implementation?

Yes. The scope can cover the target design, migration sequence and the decisions your engineers need to carry out the work. Andreas can also work alongside your team during implementation where agreed.

Can we include hardening in the migration?

Yes. An Active Directory and Entra ID security review can identify privileged accounts and settings that need attention. Your team can then decide which changes to make before the move and which to include in it.

How do we decide which permissions carry over?

Andreas works with your application and service owners to establish what people and systems need in the new environment. Access governance can help define roles, give each permission an owner and set expectations for future reviews.
