Design an identity environment your team can run

Where identities are managed, how applications sign in and who can approve access all affect what your team will have to manage after a migration. Wardmark helps you make those decisions before accounts and applications move.

  • Active Directory consolidation. Bring directories together and decide which accounts, groups and permissions need to move.
  • Cloud and hybrid identity. Design how Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID work together or move application sign-in to Entra ID.
  • Zero Trust architecture. Define how access is checked and limit permissions to what each person or service needs to do its work.
