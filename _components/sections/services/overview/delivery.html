Know what the engagement will deliver

Before work starts, you and Andreas agree on which systems are in scope, what decisions the work needs to support and what your team will receive. For a review, that might be a prioritised list of changes with the reasons behind them. For a migration, it might be a design and a plan that your application owners can review before anything moves.

When implementation is in scope, Andreas plans changes with the people who run the affected systems. At handover, your team gets a record of what changed and what it needs to maintain.
