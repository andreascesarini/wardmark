Frequently asked questions

Can one engagement cover more than one service area?

Yes. A directory consolidation can include a review of administrator access, for example. The scope sets out which parts of the environment Andreas will review, design or change.

What if we already have findings from a security review?

Andreas can review the findings with your team, work out which changes to make first and agree on the design or implementation work needed to address them.

Can you work with the identity tools we already use?

Yes. Wardmark is vendor-independent. Andreas starts with how your current tools are used and what your team needs to control. That helps you decide which changes you can make with the tools you already have.

Can the work support audit preparation?

Yes. The scope can include documenting who holds privileged access, who approves it and how it’s reviewed. Your team can use that evidence to explain how access is managed.
