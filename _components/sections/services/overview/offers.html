Where Wardmark can help

Active Directory & Entra ID Security

Find out which accounts hold privileged access, which permissions are still needed and which settings need hardening. A review can give your team a map of administrator access and a prioritised plan for removing unnecessary permissions and improving configuration.

Identity Architecture & Migration

Design a new identity environment, consolidate directories or move applications to Microsoft Entra ID authentication. The work can cover Zero Trust design and a migration plan that shows which applications depend on the current setup, what needs to move and in what order.

Access Governance

Give people and services access based on what they need to do. Define roles, decide who approves access and set out when permissions should be reviewed. Your team gets a role model and a record of access decisions it can use during routine reviews and audits.

AI Agent Identity Readiness

Work out what each AI agent needs to access before connecting it to business systems. A readiness review can help you define what an agent is allowed to do, name who is responsible for its access and decide how its activity will be reviewed.
