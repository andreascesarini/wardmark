Secondary offer

Show how this service fits a specific customer need.

This page is useful when a business has more than one meaningful path into its offer. It can describe a recurring service, a specialized package, a common add-on, or a customer segment.

Use the copy to clarify fit: who should choose this service, what makes it different from the primary service, and what the first conversation should cover.

  • Package, plan, or visit structure
  • What customers should prepare
  • Common add-ons or upgrade paths
